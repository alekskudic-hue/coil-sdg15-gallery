The problem we see.

Understanding electronic waste and why it matters.

OUR CHALLENGE

Electronic waste is growing.

Every year, people throw away millions of electronic devices. Phones, computers and batteries contain materials that can harm the environment if they are not disposed of correctly.

Instead of buying new devices all the time, we should repair, reuse and recycle our electronics. Small changes can make a big difference.
